About Guess the Cube

A quick way to train your sense of perspective. Each round shows one face of a cube in a random orientation, drawn in true perspective. Your job is to sketch the rest of the cube.

How to play

  1. Look at the blue face. The cube always extends away from you, behind it.
  2. Sketch the missing edges freehand. That's a depth edge from each corner, plus the square of the far face.
  3. Press Reveal to see the real cube over your sketch. Dashed lines are edges hidden behind the cube.
  4. Press Next cube and go again.

Controls

  • Enter / Space: reveal, or next cube
  • Ctrl/⌘ + Z: undo last stroke
  • C: clear the sketch
  • Mouse, touch and pen all work.

Scoring

Your strokes are compared with the edges you had to guess. Stray lines cost points, and so do edges you leave out. Retracing the blue face neither helps nor hurts. A sketch that adds nothing to the blue face scores 0, and a perfect trace scores 100. Hidden edges count only if Score hidden edges is on in Settings.
